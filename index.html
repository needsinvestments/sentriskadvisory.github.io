How the two practices work together on one shipment

The terms you sell on decide who carries the risk, and your insurance has to follow that line. When two separate advisors handle each side, the gaps surface during a claim. We handle both from the start.

Export-Import AdvisoryInsurance Advisory

The delivery term is chosen, and with it who arranges transport and when risk passes to the buyer.

Sale terms agreed

Cover is arranged for the party that actually bears the risk, on the basis the contract requires.

Invoice, packing list and shipping documents are prepared and checked.

Goods dispatched

Insured value, goods description and voyage details are declared consistently with those documents.

Forwarders, transporters and customs agents are coordinated to one plan.

Goods in transit

Marine cargo cover remains in force for the full journey and its conditions.

Contract and shipping records show what was agreed and what was delivered.

Loss or dispute

The claim is supported by those same records, which helps it settle faster.

Both practices work from the same facts, so your contract and your cover never contradict each other.
